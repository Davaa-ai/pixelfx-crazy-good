# Anime lab catalog

12 effects. Render any of them with `node anime/run.mjs render <id> --gif`.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Slayer mark | `demon-slayer/slayer-mark` | loop 1.6s | 72×116 | A heartbeat pulse: crimson veins race over the body and a mark flares on the brow, red light breathes round him and pink-white steam pours off, rising. |
